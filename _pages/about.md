I am a Master’s student at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences, New York University](https://cims.nyu.edu/dynamic/) and a  Research Volunteer at the Biomedical Data Sciences Hub (Bio-DaSH) lab in NYU Langone Health, where I work with [Prof. Gustavo Stolovitzky](https://med.nyu.edu/faculty/gustavo-a-stolovitzky) on benchmarking clinical AI tools and making them more robust to temporal and demographic biases.

Previously, I was a Visiting Research Fellow at [CVIT, IIIT Hyderabad](https://cvit.iiit.ac.in), advised by 
[Prof. C.V. Jawahar](https://cvit.iiit.ac.in/?view=article&id=8&catid=24) and [Prof. Anand Mishra](https://anandmishra22.github.io) where I advanced lecture presentation understanding using synthetic slide generation. We published our findings at ICDAR 2025 (Oral) and made the dataset and generation pipeline openly accessible.

In summer 2023, I interned at [Wells Fargo](https://www.linkedin.com/company/wellsfargo) , developing semantic-aware audio transcriptions for onboarding presentations tailored to over 15,000 visually impaired employees worldwide. During my undergraduate studies at  [SVNIT, Surat](https://www.svnit.ac.in), I worked with [Prof. Rupa G Mehta](https://scholar.google.co.in/citations?user=DSN0Cw0AAAAJ&hl=en) to enhance the explainability of thoracic disease predictors through clinical ontology integration.

My current interest areas are in Clinical AI Reasoning & Safety, Mechanistic Interpretibility, Multimodal Synthetic Data Generation, and Optimizing Inference pipelines of LLMs/VLMs.  

Beyond research, I’m a former competitive swimmer from India, served as Chapter Head of the ACM Student Chapter at NIT Surat, and was an editor for its official publication club, Renesa. These days I am into running and museum-hopping in and around NYC.
