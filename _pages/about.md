Hey there! I am a Software Engineer at [Google](https://about.google), in the Search division, working on improving [AI Search (AI Overviews / AI Mode)](https://search.google/ai-in-search/). I recently graduated with a Master of Computer Science degree from the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences, New York University](https://cims.nyu.edu/dynamic/) where I was advised by [Prof. Gustavo Stolovitzky](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=yb-POEcAAAAJ&hl=en) for my Master's Thesis on proposing a novel calibration method to benchmark clinical AI tools under distribution shift. I was also fortunate to work with [Prof. Eunsol Choi](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=6wulN88AAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate) at [CILVR Lab](https://wp.nyu.edu/cilvr/) on mechansitic interpretability of attention heads in transformer-based LLMs.
Previously, I was a Visiting Research Fellow at [CVIT, IIIT Hyderabad](https://cvit.iiit.ac.in), advised by [Prof. C.V. Jawahar](https://cvit.iiit.ac.in/?view=article&id=8&catid=24) and [Prof. Anand Mishra](https://anandmishra22.github.io) where I advanced document intelligence through AI-generated lecture slides (ICDAR 2025). I received my undergraduate degree from [SVNIT, Surat](https://www.svnit.ac.in) and interned at [Wells Fargo](https://www.linkedin.com/company/wellsfargo) working in the Technology Innovation team.

My current interest areas are in AI Safety and Alignment, Multimodal Reasoning, and  AI for Research & Scientific Discovery.  

In the extra-curicullar realm, I’m a former competitive swimmer representing the state of Gujarat in four All-India Swimming National Championships. I have served as Chapter Head of the ACM Student Chapter at NIT Surat, and was an editor for its official publication club, Renesa. These days I am into running and hiking in-and-around the SF Bay Area.
